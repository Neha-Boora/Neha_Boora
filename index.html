I am a doctoral student at Texas A&M University. I completed my master's in biotechnology at West Virginia State University, where I focused on plant genetics and genomics.

I study how plants respond to stress at the level of the genome. My current work is on the molecular and physiological basis of glufosinate resistance in Amaranthus tuberculatus, and on extrachromosomal circular DNA (eccDNA) in Amaranthus palmeri.

Before this I characterized an F-box gene involved in arsenic tolerance in Arabidopsis thaliana—a positive regulator first identified in my lab. Working through its pathway, I found a further regulator that responds to arsenite stress.

I like working across the whole chain of evidence: molecular and analytical bench work, controlled greenhouse and field experiments, and the computational analysis that ties a phenotype back to a sequence.
